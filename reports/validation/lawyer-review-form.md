# STARE sample memo: review form for a lawyer or law-faculty member

Thank you for reviewing this memo. STARE is a final-year student project (Group 36, Bennett University, CBCA301
Capstone). It drafts research memos on US federal employment-discrimination law and checks every citation against
court records before a lawyer sees it. We would like a legal professional's view on whether the output is accurate
and useful. The review takes about 20 minutes.

**What to read:** `sample-memo-ndtex-title7.docx` in this folder. It is a draft IRAC memo for a fictional Title VII
retaliation and disparate-treatment complaint filed in the Northern District of Texas. All names and personal
details in the complaint are invented. The memo was produced by STARE in its offline mode, which builds each rule
statement from a verified quotation of the opinion.

**This is research material, not legal advice.** Nothing in the memo should be relied on for a real matter.
